Locked-off shot, 6 seconds, real time, no camera movement. The white spark is already at the apex of the arc and stays at exactly the same spot in the frame for the whole shot: it does not travel forward, does not drop and does not leave the arc. It only glows steadily and slowly shrinks to a tiny, bright point of light. The trail stays attached to the spark. High-altitude wind slowly bends, twists and thins the lower part of the trail, on the left side of the frame. The exposure, the light and the sky colours stay constant: bright daylight, pale blue at the bottom and deep blue-indigo at the top, with only a very slight deepening of the blue at the very top of the frame. The sky never turns black or purple. Nothing else moves and nothing enters the frame. No text, no logos, no insignia, no emblems, no watermark.
