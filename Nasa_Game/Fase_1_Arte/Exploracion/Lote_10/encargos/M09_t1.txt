8 seconds. The camera looks straight down and descends slowly and steadily, with no rotation: the ground comes closer and drifts slowly toward the bottom of the frame. The long four-legged shadow grows larger and its edges sharpen. Thin rays of dust shoot outward along the ground in perfectly straight lines, fast and flat, crisp as if shot with a very short shutter, with no cloud and no swirling, because there is no air. At the seventh second all motion stops at once: the descent stops, the shadow is still and sharp, and the dust is gone. The last second is completely still. The craft is never visible, only its shadow. No footprints, no flags, no people. No text, no logos, no insignia, no emblems, no watermark.
